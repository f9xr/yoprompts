About

A prompt library, not a content farm

YoPrompts is built and published by F9XR Team. Every prompt here is written by hand, run against real models, and shipped with an honest read-time estimate.

Why this exists

Most prompt content online is filler. It restates the model's own documentation, pads it to a word count, and buries the two lines that actually mattered. You spend thirty seconds reading it, learn nothing you could not have typed yourself, and close the tab.

YoPrompts takes the opposite position. A prompt is a small piece of engineering, and like any engineering it is either tested or it is fiction. Every prompt in this library states the models it was run against, what it does well, where it falls down, and what to change when the output is wrong. If a prompt does not earn its place, we do not publish it.

How a prompt gets published

  1. Someone identifies a task people repeatedly botch with an AI assistant — not a task the model handles fine unaided.
  2. A draft is written and run against two or more models, with real input rather than a toy example.
  3. The prompt is revised until the failure mode is gone. The most common fix is an explicit instruction not to invent things, because hallucinated specifics are the reason most output is unusable.
  4. Usage steps, tips, an example output and the questions people actually ask are written alongside it. Those four sections are not filler — they are usually where the value is.
  5. The difficulty and read-time fields are checked against the real length of the prompt, not copied from the previous entry.

What we will not do

  • We will not fabricate testimonials. If there are user quotes on this site they came from a real person who agreed to be named. We do not write our own.
  • We will not pad word counts. A prompt that does the job in forty words does not get stretched to four hundred.
  • We will not sell your attention. Analytics are consent-gated. Decline the banner and no tracking script loads at all — see the cookie policy.
  • We will not claim to be a research lab. This is an independent prompt library, not affiliated with any AI provider.

Corrections

Models change. A prompt tuned against one version of a model can quietly degrade against the next, and we cannot test every release of every tool. If a prompt here stopped working for you, that is a real bug and we want to hear about it — see contact or submit a prompt.

The short version

Hand-written, model-tested prompts with honest documentation. Free to copy, no account, no upsell.
