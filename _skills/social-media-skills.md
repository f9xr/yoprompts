Social Media Skills chains its sub-skills around a voice-builder foundation. That skill interviews the writer, reads three to five samples, and produces a persistent voice profile the other skills use, so hooks, posts, carousels, and newsletters stay consistent.

The suite covers content ideation with a format matrix, hook generation, framework-driven post formatting, post scoring against real performance data, reels scripting, Gemini carousels and infographics, newsletter voice, niche research, and LinkedIn profile optimization.

A few sub-skills call external services. Reels scripting needs Apify and a Google AI key, while niche research relies on Claude for Chrome.
