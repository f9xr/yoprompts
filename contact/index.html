Contact

Talk to a human

One inbox, read by the people who write the prompts. Email is the fastest route — there is no form, no ticket queue and no chatbot.

Email

hello@f9xr.org

That address reaches F9XR Team. It is the same inbox behind f9xr.org, so anything site-specific is best titled YoPrompts so it does not get lost.

What to write about

  • A prompt that stopped working. Tell us which prompt, which model, and what it produced instead. Model releases break things silently and we cannot test every version of every tool, so this is genuinely useful.
  • A factual correction. If something here is wrong, say so plainly and we will fix the page.
  • Reusing a prompt. The prompts are free to copy and use, including commercially. See the terms for the exact position, or just ask if your case is unusual.
  • A prompt you want to contribute. Read submit a prompt first — it explains what makes an entry worth publishing.

Response time

This is a small independent project, not a support desk. Treat a week as a reasonable expectation and assume sooner rather than later. We are not going to pretend otherwise by promising 24-hour turnaround.

What we cannot help with

We do not have access to your account at any AI provider, cannot see your prompts or outputs, and cannot recover anything for you. If a model refused to do something, that decision sits with the provider.
