Copy Editing works on copy that already exists. It runs structured sweeps for clarity, voice, proof, risk, and conversion instead of rewriting the whole page from scratch.

Each sweep has a narrow job: tighten sentences, remove corporate language, replace generic claims with evidence, move the call to action into view, and delete patterns that make the text read as machine-written.

A request to fix one line stays a point edit. Larger requests get a marked-up revision and a short list of what changed and why.
