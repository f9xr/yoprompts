This skill writes and rewrites marketing copy for homepages, landing pages, pricing pages, feature pages, and about pages. It starts by diagnosing whether the brief is blank, a real draft with a voice to preserve, or a narrow point edit, then changes only as much as that situation allows.

Every line runs through a three-question gate: can the reader picture it, can it be proven true or false, and could a competitor write the same sentence. Claims that cannot be supported are flagged as missing evidence rather than invented.

It also ships a blacklist of AI tells, including contrast reveals, negation lists, trailing pile-ons, self-answered questions, and stock openers, so the draft does not read as generated.
