Cold Email writes outbound that is specific to the prospect and the offer. It covers subject lines, opening lines, follow-ups, LinkedIn messages, and multichannel cadences.

The skill starts from a verified list and a real reason to reach out. It recommends secondary sending domains, inbox warmup, open-tracking off, and stopping every channel as soon as a reply arrives.

It also handles objections and reply classification so the next message is based on what the prospect actually said. List building and account research stay in the separate prospecting skill.
