AI Search Optimization audits content for answer engines rather than only classic rankings. It checks whether AI crawlers can reach the page, whether entities and claims are unambiguous, and whether passages can stand alone as answers.

The skill covers citability, source attribution, brand mentions, retrieval-friendly headings, and the difference between traffic from AI Overviews and traffic from chat assistants. It also explains which claims need first-party evidence.

Recommendations are grouped into quick wins, medium-effort changes, and high-impact structural work, with a note on what cannot be verified without access to each platform.
