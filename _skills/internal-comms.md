Internal Comms is a writing skill for the messages that sit between a decision and a team: status reports, leadership updates, project announcements, incident notes, and FAQs.

It starts from the audience and the decision the reader needs to make, then chooses the format. A leadership update is not a blog post with a different salutation, and an FAQ is not a place to hide an unresolved question.

The skill includes guidance on tone, structure, and what to leave out. Give it real context and a sample of the company's existing voice; the output improves more from that than from a longer list of adjectives.
