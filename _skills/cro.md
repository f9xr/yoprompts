Conversion Rate Optimization reviews a page or form against its stated goal. It looks at message match, clarity, friction, proof, hierarchy, and the path to the primary action.

The output separates quick wins from high-impact changes and test ideas. Copy alternatives are included where wording is the likely blocker, and each recommendation names the evidence behind it.

The skill can work from a URL, a screenshot description, or pasted copy. It does not invent analytics; when traffic and conversion data are missing, it says which measurements would settle the question.
