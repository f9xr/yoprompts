### 🎨 1. DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL STYLE
- Theme: Ultra-modern, premium tech-forward SaaS aesthetic.
- Color Palette: 
  * Backgrounds: Pure White (#FFFFFF) for body text readability, paired with light, muted pastel purple/lavender (#F3E8FF) block backgrounds.
  * Primary Accents: Deep Deep Royal Violet/Indigo (#2E1065) and Electric Royal Purple (#6B21A8) used for dark container blocks, hero elements, footer, and heavy contrast.
  * Secondary Accents: Vibrant Neon Mint (#86EFAC) or Soft Cyber Sage (#BBF7D0) for specialized status badges or testimonial borders.
- Typography: High-contrast, clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Satoshi). 
  * Hero Headlines: Bold, massive, tightly tracked, using multi-tonal text gradients (e.g., solid dark purple transitioning into semi-transparent or lighter lavender text).
- Component Styling: Rounded corners (border-radius: 16px to 24px). Soft, diffused box shadows for floating card elements. Avoid harsh black borders. Use precise, generous vertical padding to create breathing room between structural sections.
