Marketing Skills is a bundle for technical marketers and founders rather than a single workflow. The product-marketing skill runs first and writes shared positioning, audience, and product context that the rest of the set reads, so copy, pages, and campaigns start from the same brief.

The 43 sub-skills cover conversion optimization, copywriting and editing, SEO audits, programmatic SEO, AI search visibility, schema, analytics, paid acquisition, email, pricing, onboarding, referrals, and sales enablement.

Installing the bundle adds every skill. You can also install one at a time, such as `coreyhaines31/marketingskills@copywriting`, when only a single workflow is needed.
