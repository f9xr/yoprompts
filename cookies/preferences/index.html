Change your mind at any time. Accepting loads aggregate analytics; rejecting keeps every third-party script off. The site works identically either way.

If nothing is shown above, you have not answered the banner yet and no analytics code is running. You can decide below.

Change it

Choosing does two things: it saves the decision in this browser's local storage, and it loads or unloads Google Analytics immediately. No page reload is needed.

Analytics are Google Analytics 4 and are used only for aggregate page-view counts. See the privacy policy and cookie policy for detail.

What each choice means

  • Accept. Google Analytics loads and may set _ga and _ga_<container-id> for up to two years. We see aggregate counts, never individual visitors.
  • Reject. No analytics script is requested, no analytics cookie is set, and no data is shared with Google for measurement. Search, filters and copy all still work.

Resetting completely

To clear the saved decision as well as any analytics cookies, clear browser data for this site in your browser settings. The consent banner will then reappear on your next visit.
