Cookie Policy

What this site sets, in plain terms. The default answer is “nothing”, and that is not a figure of speech.

Last updated 4 October 2026

By default: nothing

Load this site and decline the consent banner - or ignore it entirely - and no analytics code executes, no third-party script runs, and no cookie is set. The prompt library, search and copy buttons all work with tracking fully off. There is nothing to opt out of because nothing was ever switched on.

What is always set

One value in localStorage, not a cookie:

  • yoprompts_consent - records that you answered the banner, either granted or denied.

Its only job is to stop the banner reappearing on every page. It never leaves your browser, contains no identifier, and cannot be used to recognise you. Deleting your browser data for this site resets it and the banner returns.

What is set if you accept

Accepting the banner loads Google Analytics 4, which may then set the following. Rejecting keeps all of it off.

  • _ga - distinguishes visitors for aggregate page-view counting. Two years.
  • _ga_ - persists the above across sessions, so returning visits are not double counted. Two years.

Google may additionally use IP addresses, user agents and device signals to derive approximate country and device type. That is processed by Google, not by us. See the privacy policy for the full breakdown.

Declining without a banner

If you already answered, you can change your answer at any time on the cookie preferences page. The change takes effect straight away, without a reload.

You can suppress the banner and the analytics entirely by using a content blocker, enabling your browser's Do Not Track setting, or installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. The banner also stays hidden if your browser already signals a Do Not Track preference.

Fonts

Type is loaded from Google Fonts. Serving a font requires your browser to contact Google, which means Google sees your IP address and user agent as part of delivering the file. Fonts are not a tracking cookie and no identifier is set, but it is a third-party request, and it happens whether or not you accept analytics. Self-hosting the fonts would remove it and is a reasonable thing for us to do next.

Embedded content

Card artwork is inline SVG, so there are no iframes, no embedded players and no social widgets loading third-party scripts. That is deliberate.

Changing your mind

Clear this site's browser data and the consent banner reappears, or email hello@f9xr.org if you would rather we treated your site data as blocked at the source.
