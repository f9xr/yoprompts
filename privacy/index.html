Legal

Privacy Policy

The short version: there is nothing to sign up for, nothing stored in a database, and no tracking of you unless you actively agree to it.

Last updated 4 October 2026

Who runs this site

YoPrompts is published by F9XR Team (f9xr.org). For any privacy question, email hello@f9xr.org.

The honest summary

  • No account, no login, no user database.
  • No server-side code and no server-side logging of visitors.
  • Your prompt text never leaves your browser unless you copy it yourself.
  • Analytics are off until you click accept.

What we collect ourselves

We operate a static site. There is no application server, no database, and no first-party analytics of our own. When you press Copy on a prompt, that happens entirely in your browser via the clipboard API. Your prompt text is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and see which prompts are read. It is strictly opt-in: a consent banner appears on your first visit and no analytics script loads until you accept. If you decline, or dismiss the banner without choosing, analytics stays off permanently.

Where you do accept, Google Analytics may collect:

  • Pages viewed and time on page, in aggregate.
  • Approximate location at country level, and device/browser information, both derived from your IP address and user agent.
  • A cookie or similar identifier, set only after your consent.

We do not use Google Analytics to build advertising audiences, and we have not enabled Google Signals or any advertising-features personalisation. We look at aggregate counts only - never at identifiable visitors.

Google's own handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Data Processing Terms. To decline without seeing a banner, enable your browser's “Do Not Track” or a content blocker, or see the cookie policy.

Cookies and local storage

Before you choose, we store one item in your browser's localStorage: your consent choice, so the banner does not reappear on every page. That value never leaves your device and cannot identify you. Full detail is on the cookie policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the request data required to deliver a page - your IP address and user agent - in order to serve it, and retains it in its own privacy statement. That processing is necessary for the site to exist and is not under our control.

Third-party links

Prompt pages link out to AI providers such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini so you can paste and run the prompt. Following one of those links takes you to a third party under their own privacy policy, over which we have no control. We do not receive whatever you do there.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no identifiable data about you, there is normally nothing to export, correct or erase. To find out what Google holds about your visit, use Google's My Activity and Analytics opt-out tools. To withdraw consent here, clear your browser storage for this site and the banner will reappear.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page changes with it. We do not currently send email, so we cannot notify you directly.
