Local SEO reviews the signals that decide map-pack and local organic visibility. It starts with business category and service area, then checks name, address, and phone consistency across the site and major citations.

The skill inspects Google Business Profile fields, review patterns, local schema, location page depth, and internal links between locations and services. Multi-location businesses get a structure review so pages do not compete with each other.

Output is prioritized by likely impact and effort, with separate fixes for the website, the profile, and third-party listings.
