Writing Guidelines checks documentation and prose against the rules in Vercel's writing handbook. It starts with content type and purpose, then reviews voice, headings, lists, code samples, and sentence-level clarity.

The typography pass catches banned punctuation and formatting, including em dashes, curly quotes, ellipses, units, and number style. The workflow section covers planning, review, and disclosure of AI assistance.

Findings are tied to specific passages with a suggested rewrite, so the review produces an edit rather than a general score.
