Programmatic SEO covers the full path from keyword pattern to indexation. It starts with demand and data: how many unique combinations exist, where the volume sits, and whether the data behind each page is proprietary, product-derived, licensed, or public.

The skill documents twelve playbooks, including templates, curation, comparisons, locations, personas, integrations, glossaries, directories, and profiles. It then walks through template design, hub-and-spoke internal linking, per-page indexation rules, and a pre-launch quality checklist.

It is explicit about the failure mode: swapping a city name into identical copy produces thin pages. Differentiated data and conditional sections are treated as requirements, not polish.
