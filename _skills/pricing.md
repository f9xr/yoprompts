Pricing Strategy works through business context, value and competition, current performance, and goals before recommending a number. It treats price as a decision to learn from rather than a permanent guess.

The skill covers value metrics, packaging, free plans, trials, annual versus monthly billing, and common psychological traps. It also explains when to use Van Westendorp or MaxDiff research and what each method can actually tell you.

Pricing pages get a separate audit for human clarity and machine readability, since AI assistants increasingly shortlist tools from those pages.
