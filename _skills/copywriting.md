Copywriting starts by diagnosing the brief: a blank page, an existing voice to match, or a single line that needs work. It then writes only what the situation calls for, from a headline to a full page.

The skill enforces supportable claims, specific value propositions, and a direct voice. It avoids stock AI patterns such as balanced reveals, triplet lists, and sentences that keep adding comma clauses.

Page-specific guidance covers homepages, landing pages, pricing, features, about pages, and calls to action. For existing copy, the separate copy-editing skill is the better fit.
