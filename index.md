# YoPrompts

Ready-made AI prompts by F9XR Team. Copy a prompt, paste it into ChatGPT,
Claude, Gemini, or whatever you already use, and get a better result than
writing the request from scratch.

> Think. Prompt. Create. Copy. Paste. Repeat.

- **Prompts** — a library of tested, ready-to-copy prompts with usage notes,
  FAQs, and the models each one was checked against.
- **Categories** — browse prompts by what you are trying to get done.
- **Guides** — short write-ups on prompting technique and tooling.

Start here: <https://f9xr.org/yoprompts/>
